Mini Biography: Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an esteemed Indian scientist and administrator who served as the 11th President of India. Widely known as the "Missile Man of India," he was deeply loved by the public, especially students, for his humility and dedication to the nation.

Early Life

Born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Kalam came from a humble background. He faced financial hardships early in life and distributed newspapers to support his family, yet his profound dedication to education helped him graduate in aeronautical engineering.

Important Achievement

Dr. Kalam played a pivotal role in leading India's major space and defense projects. His leadership during the Pokhran-II nuclear tests solidified India's position on the global stage. He worked with unwavering focus throughout his career, demonstrating a remarkable level of patriotism that inspired millions. Note: He spent over four decades as a scientist at DRDO and ISRO.

Many believe that he was always a politician, but he began his career in politics he was a lifelong scientist who only entered public office later in life. Beyond politics, he was passionate about science education, often explaining how a simple molecule like H2O supports life, or how 102 small steps can lead to a giant leap in development.

Words That Inspire

Dr. Kalam always urged the youth to think big, famously saying, Dream is not that which you see while sleeping it is something that does not let you sleep.

"If you want to shine like a sun, first burn like a sun."
