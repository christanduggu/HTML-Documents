Mini Biography: Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian scientist and politician who served as the 11th President of India. Renowned as the "People's President," he was deeply loved for his humility and dedication to inspiring the youth.

Early Life

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Kalam came from a humble background. He overcame severe financial hardships in his childhood by selling newspapers to support his family and pursue his education in physics and aerospace engineering.

Important Achievement

He played a pivotal role in developing India's missile programs, earning him the title of the "Missile Man of India." His leadership led to the successful deployment of the Agni and Prithvi missiles. He possessed an unyielding vision for self-reliance, which transformed India's defense capabilities.

Note: He also served as the Chief Scientific Adviser to the Prime Minister during the Pokhran-II nuclear tests.

Some people mistakenly believe he started his career as a politician, but he was actually a politician scientist first.

Dr. Kalam always believed that science, from understanding the chemistry of H2O (water) to launching rockets 102 (10 squared) miles into the sky, should serve humanity.

Words That Inspire

Dr. Kalam always emphasized the power of dreaming, stating that Dreams are not those which we see while sleeping, they are something that do not let us sleep.

"If you want to shine like a sun, first burn like a sun."
